About

This is not your ordinary A.I. event.

It's a Human Intelligence experience — bold, immersive, and unapologetically human-first. Because the future of work deserves more than another AI conference.

This year the Los Angeles Regional Consortium is flipping the AI narrative by highlighting and celebrating the irreplaceable role of human intelligence in an A.I.-driven world. The festival will demonstrate how human traits such as critical thinking, empathy, creativity, judgment, and ethics complement and elevate the use of artificial intelligence in education and workforce development.

Why this festival?

Let's celebrate the opportunities that give our LA talent pool and employers the edge when working alongside AI.

  • AI may help write the résumé, but humans still evaluate potential.
  • AI may help prepare for the interview, but humans still build connection.
  • AI may support teaching and learning, but humans still inspire growth.
  • AI may generate possibilities, but humans still exercise judgment and create opportunity.
  • AI may automate tasks, but humans still build trust.

Together, these become one conversation: Who do we become as AI becomes more capable? What makes humans worth hiring in an AI-enabled world?

Real Talk. Real People. Real Urgency.

A.I. is already transforming classrooms, workplaces, and entire industries. Yet most conferences are skipping the most important question: how do we, as educators, students, and industry leaders, respond today? The festival brings together diverse voices from education and the workforce to:

  • Address education-to-workforce alignment like no one else is
  • Elevate voices too often left out of the AI conversation — especially students
  • Co-create solutions to the AI-induced shifts we're experiencing in real time
  • Capture actionable data to inform policies, practices, and the future of work
  • Empower people to understand A.I.'s impact and reclaim agency — so we feel in control of A.I., not controlled by it

The Human Intelligence Festival isn't just a conversation — it's a real-time research engine.
